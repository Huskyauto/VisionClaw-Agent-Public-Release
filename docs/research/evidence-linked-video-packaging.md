# Native adaptations from the YouTube packaging prompt and REA trading page

## Adopted
- Three different title/thumbnail pairs drafted in the existing BWB script call.
- Viewer, promise, selection rationale and exact final-narration references.
- Shared local/CI review: bounded shape, valid scene/quote references, repeated
  thumbnail/title copy (including partial/reordered copies), title
  numeric claims present in quotations, distinct titles, selected pair matches
  the actual script's title/thumbnail prompt. No extra model calls/retry loop.
- Review in builder logs, result sidecars, delivery metadata and email output;
  weekly approval email contains the advisory summary in HTML (escaped) and
  plain text.
- Research generation/evaluator instructions separate observed, inferred,
  unknown and falsifier, preserving the existing RESULT parser shape.

## Evidence limits

This is an owner-directed workflow improvement, not measured channel growth.
Literal quotation matching cannot prove semantic support, original-source truth,
visual truth or factual accuracy. Evidence is the final spoken narration, not
independently authenticated original footage. Existing weight and source guards
are unchanged. A `traceable` report still requires editorial review and never
approves uploading. The subject hash identifies the reviewed script text, not
rendered video bytes or viewer acceptance.

Thumbnail text is a concept only; existing image rendering does not apply an
overlay. No image was generated during implementation. Three candidate angles
and research evidence categories are prompt guidance, not an independent
behavioral-quality guarantee.

Old/manual scripts without optional packaging continue to render/deliver with an
explicit needs-review report. Cached scripts/delivery emails are not silently
rewritten or resent. No new upfront failure or expensive retry is introduced.

## Deliberately not adopted

No REA toolkit, binary worker, trading bot, financial-strategy claim, schema,
provider, agent fleet, analytics service, auto-upload permission or evaluator
threshold changes. No claim of proprietary strategy recovery or trading profit.

## Future evaluation

Use actual comparable YouTube impression/click data WITH watch time and early
retention; record audience/source/topic differences and uncertainty. An AI
editorial preference is not an A/B result. The current changes do not fetch,
invent or automate analytics. Research adoption still needs independent held-out
cases, failure cases, costs of failed attempts/retries and the existing approval
and deterministic evaluation controls.
