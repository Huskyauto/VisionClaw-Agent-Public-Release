circumstances when a reassessment may be necessary [L34-L100].\n\n**(d) Requirements.** The Contractor shall—\n(1)(i) Maintain a current CMMC status at the required level for systems compliance [L112].\n(5) If status is Conditional, close out a POA&M to achieve Final status [L114].\n\n*Note: Level 1 (Self) is specifically referenced as an acceptable CMMC level in paragraph
